    second list. It is the same text, in German or English, and it is always the text
    of the running version.

    The questions live in `src/util/translations.py`. This page is rendered from them,
    so the site and the bot cannot say different things.

## Still stuck?

Use `/feedback` in Discord. It reaches us directly and you can write freely.

Tell us three things:

- what you were trying to do
- what happened instead
- a screenshot if you have one
